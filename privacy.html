Hollowhold Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Hollowhold is a tower defense game made by Kappish AB in Sweden. This page explains what happens to your data when you play it. The short version: the game collects nothing about you.

What the game collects

Nothing. Hollowhold has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not need an internet connection, and it sends no data to us or to anyone else while you play.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (your hero, your journey, your settings and what you have unlocked) is saved in files on your own device. They never leave it. Deleting the game deletes them.

Feedback you choose to send

When you finish the first act, the game offers a short survey. If you choose to send it, it opens your own email app with your answers filled in, and nothing is sent until you press Send. Then we receive your email address and your answers, and use them only to improve the game. We do not share them, and we delete them when the test is over. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

TestFlight

While Hollowhold is in testing, it is delivered through Apple's TestFlight. Apple may share crash reports, screenshots and feedback you send through TestFlight with us, as described in Apple's privacy policy. We use them only to fix and improve the game.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and contact

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Write to hej@kappish.se. If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
